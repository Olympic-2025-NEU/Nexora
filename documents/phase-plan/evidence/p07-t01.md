## P07-T01 criteria proven at contract level

- All six types have an explicit frozen field/status/action matrix and matching schema status/type enums. The goods issue graph alone has approval states; all other types directly confirm from Draft. `rejected`, `confirmed`, and `cancelled` have no outgoing actions. Draft `edit` is a self-transition; version CAS remains application work.
- `documentStockEffect` is `none` for every status before confirm and for transfer/sale even after confirm. D14 ownership and mutually exclusive internal receipt/partner receiving paths are documented; this is **not** a measurement of real stock movement. Tests reject invalid transitions, self-decision, missing bridge approval/hash/scope, corrections targeting Draft or missing reason, receiving overage, wrong-purpose/double-linked issue, and mismatched per-line lot/quantity.
- Approval state, document status and movement ownership are separate. Contract requires the existing bridge to verify current line hash, live warehouse scope and current `If-Match` when confirming, with no auto-expiry in V1. Cancelled documents cannot be resurrected by a late decision. Pending cancellation must tombstone the approval request when integrated.
- Correction is a new same-type document with a confirmed predecessor and reason. Guards allow correction of correction but refuse a second active correction for the same predecessor; the new document follows its own lifecycle and uses its own lines. Stocktake variance is specified as counted minus balance read under lock at confirm; zero delta creates no movement.
- API and schema blueprints document exact field matrix, planned 409/422 error mapping, `Idempotency-Key`, and atomic confirm postconditions. Phase-07 checklist rows 1 and 4 are checked for **contract definition only**; runtime invariants in those rows remain unverified until their owners complete.
