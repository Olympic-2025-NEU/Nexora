# G08 — Local traceability, Passport and QR

Date: 2026-11-24. Branch: `phase-08`. Scope: P08-T01–T08 local M04; cross-instance federation is P09.

## Gate result

**AC-08 passes at the local automated gate.** A confirmed M03 goods receipt produces stock movement and a versioned outbox message; the trace consumer creates one locally verified source event; backward/forward queries return that document; an approved public policy builds a Passport; its issued opaque QR URL decodes from PNG and resolves to the allowlisted public payload. Token revocation immediately removes access. This is an automated disposable-database and PNG decoder test, not a physical phone scan or a deployed HTTPS smoke test. P11 must check the latter on the demo environment.

| M03 source → trace | `P08-T08 M03 confirm flows through outbox to local trace and public QR` in `tests/platform/trace-projector.integration.test.ts`: calls the actual M03 `tradingService.create/action(confirm)`, checks Draft has no outbox, confirmed stock balance `7.000`, pending receipt outbox, one projected event with source document FK and issuer-qualified origin lot | Local source is `confirmed` and trace is `local_verified`; no event before dispatch. |
